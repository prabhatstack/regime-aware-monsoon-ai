Blueprint Section 19: One-Line Project Explanation for Judges
“Our system does not replace the weather model; it learns how NWP rainfall forecasts behave under different monsoon regimes and applies regime-aware machine-learning corrections to improve rainfall forecasts and heavy-rainfall decision support.”
Blueprint Section 17: What We Do NOT Claim
  • We do not replace NWP: We provide an ML post-processing layer on top of raw numerical models.
  • ERA5 is not direct ground truth: Used for atmospheric context & reanalysis; rain-gauge/IMD gridded data is ground truth.
  • No data leakage: We strictly split data by monsoon seasons (2021-2023 for train, 2024 held out), never random row splits.
  • Deep learning is not required: XGBoost excels on structured tabular/spatiotemporal forecast tabular features without massive GPU footprint.
  • Non-negative safeguard: Physical rainfall cannot be negative; bounded at $\max(0, \text{NWP} + \text{Error})$.
Data Ingestion & Operational Pipeline

NWP Forecast Sources: ECMWF TIGGE / NCMRWF / IMD-GFS historical archives (GRIB2 / NetCDF formats).

Atmospheric Predictors: Low-level zonal/meridional winds ($u_{850}, v_{850}$), Mean Sea Level Pressure (MSLP), 500 hPa Geopotential Height ($Z_{500}$), 2m Relative Humidity, Temperature.

District Resolution: Grid-to-district polygon area-weighted averaging across Jharkhand (Ranchi, Jamshedpur, Dhanbad, Bokaro, etc.).
